## 3. Permitted Uses (No Permission Needed)

You may use the FaradAiWire wordmark without prior written permission in the following contexts:

1. **Truthful & Nominative Reference:**
   - Describing or discussing the software (e.g., in articles, presentations, blog posts, reviews).
   - Accurately indicating compatibility or integration:
     - ✅ *"Plugin for FaradAiWire"*
     - ✅ *"FastMCP adapter compatible with FaradAiWire"*
     - ✅ *"Built with FaradAiWire Core"*

2. **Linking to the Official Project:**

## 4. Prohibited Uses (Explicitly Forbidden Without Written License)

You may **not** use the protected marks in the following manners:

1. **Naming Forks or Derivatives:**
   - If you fork or modify the software and distribute it, you must remove official branding and choose a distinct, non-confusing name for your distribution.
   - ❌ *"FaradAiWire Community Edition"*, *"FaradAiWire Cloud"*, or *"Official FaradAiWire"* for a modified fork.
   - ✅ *"Project Guard (based on FaradAiWire Core)"*.

2. **Commercial SaaS / Managed Service Misleading Claims:**
   - You may not offer a hosted commercial service or SaaS under the name "FaradAiWire" or "FAWEE" that implies official sponsorship, affiliation, or warranty from the project authors.

3. **Domain Names and Social Accounts:**
   - You may not register domain names, social media handles, or marketplace publisher accounts that contain "faradaiwire" or "fawee" as the primary identifier (e.g., `faradaiwire-cloud.com` is strictly prohibited).

4. **Logo Modifications:**
   - Modifying the official logo, changing colors, or integrating it into third-party commercial logos without authorization is prohibited.

We encourage building tools and plugins around FaradAiWire! To keep the ecosystem clear and transparent for users:
- **Recommended package names:** `mcp-security-for-faradaiwire`, `faw-plugin-auth`
- **Avoid package names:** `official-faradaiwire`, `faradaiwire-core-upstream`
